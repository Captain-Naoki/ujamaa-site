Privacy policy.

At Ujamaa, trust and data protection are part of our values. This site was designed to collect as little data as possible.

No cookies, no trackers

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies and embeds no third-party trackers. Fonts and scripts are hosted on our own server: your browsing is not shared with third parties.

Only your browser's local storage is used, to remember your theme choice (light or dark) and to avoid replaying the intro animation. This information stays on your device.

Contact form

The form sends nothing to our servers: it opens your mail app with a pre-filled email addressed to ujamaatechsolution@gmail.com. The information you send us (name, email, organisation, message) is used only to reply to you and is never sold.

Retention

Email exchanges are kept for as long as needed to handle your request, then for at most 3 years after the last contact.

Your rights

  • Right of access, rectification and erasure;
  • Right to object and to restrict processing;
  • Right to data portability.

To exercise these rights, write to ujamaatechsolution@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

Third-party services

Links to LinkedIn, Facebook, WhatsApp, Google Maps or injara.site take you to services governed by their own privacy policies.
