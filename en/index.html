(07)FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What does Ujamaa do?

Ujamaa is a tech startup and community based in Dschang, Cameroon. We build digital products — SaaS, artificial intelligence, web, mobile and desktop — for African businesses, and we showcase the continent's tech talent.

What does the word “Ujamaa” mean?

It is a Swahili word meaning extended family, community solidarity, brotherhood and collective responsibility. It sums up the way we work: together.

What is Injara?

Injara is our first product: smart recruitment software for SMEs. It retrieves CVs from the mailbox, ranks them per position with a 0–100% score and secures video interviews. Data stays encrypted on the recruiter's computer. Visit injara.site.

How can I join the community?

Email us at ujamaatechsolution@gmail.com or reach us on LinkedIn with a short introduction of your profile (development, design, data, product). Every level is welcome.

Do you work with clients outside Cameroon?

Yes. We are incubated at the Orange Digital Center Club in Dschang and work remotely with organisations across Africa and beyond.
